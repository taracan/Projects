# Impacts of COVID-19: Evaluating Lockdown Strategies, Energy Usage Shifts, Electric Vehicle Adoption, and Supply Chain Resilience

## Overview

This project explores the **multi-dimensional impacts of COVID-19** on industries, consumer behavior, and public health. Using datasets from **Kaggle, data.gov, and the COVID Tracking Project**, we analyzed how the pandemic reshaped:

* Restaurant dining patterns
* Public health outcomes under varying lockdown strategies
* Energy consumption in different states
* Electric vehicle (EV) adoption trends
* Supply chain resilience and shortages of essential goods

The project combines **data visualization, hypothesis testing, and insights** to provide a holistic view of the pandemic’s ripple effects on society and markets.

---

## Key Insights

* **Restaurant Industry**: Sharp decline in customer reviews highlighted reduced dining activity.
* **Lockdown Impact**: New York’s early lockdown slowed case growth compared to Florida’s delayed measures.
* **Energy Trends**: California’s residential electricity prices rose due to remote work, while Hawaii’s tourism decline led to reduced demand.
* **Electric Vehicles**: BEVs grew over 300% post-2020, far outpacing PHEVs, with Tesla dominating the market.
* **Supply Chain**: Panic buying in 2020 caused severe shortages of essentials like toilet paper.
* **Healthcare Facilities**: California and Texas nursing homes revealed contrasting infection vs. mortality management strategies.

## Hypotheses Tested

1. Decline in restaurant visits during early COVID-19.
2. Early lockdown (NY) slowed case growth compared to delayed lockdown (FL).
3. Divergent electricity price trends in California vs. Hawaii.
4. BEVs outpaced PHEVs post-2020, driven by technology & sustainability.
5. Panic buying caused supply shortages of essentials in 2020.
6. California vs. Texas nursing home outcomes – infection vs. mortality management.
